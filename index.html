About Sydney

Sydney Sweeney is an American actress and producer known for her versatile roles in television and film. Born in Spokane, Washington, she has become a prominent figure in the entertainment industry with critically acclaimed performances.

Best known for her roles as Cassie Howard in HBO's Euphoria and Olivia Ross in Pretty Little Liars: Original Sin, Sydney has captivated audiences worldwide with her talent and dedication to her craft.
